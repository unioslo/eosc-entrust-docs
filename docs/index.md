## Who this is for

<div class="tre-summary-grid">

<div class="tre-summary-card">

architecture patterns instead of starting from scratch.

</div>

<div class="tre-summary-card">

## <i class="fa-solid fa-user-shield tre-icon-heading"></i> Data Stewards & Security Teams

Teams responsible for compliance, risk assessment and access control who need concrete checklists and
reference policies aligned with EOSC requirements.

</div>

<div class="tre-summary-card">

## <i class="fa-solid fa-code tre-icon-heading"></i> Technical Implementers

</div>

## Getting started

<div class="tre-card-grid tre-card-grid-steps">

<div class="tre-card tre-step-card">

### <span class="tre-card-number">1</span> Understand the Blueprint

Read the EOSC-ENTRUST Blueprint overview to understand the principles, scope and terminology used across
all resources.

</div>

<div class="tre-card tre-step-card">

### <span class="tre-card-number">2</span> Assess your starting point

Use the readiness checklists to identify what governance, security and technical capabilities your
organisation already has in place.

</div>

<div class="tre-card tre-step-card">

### <span class="tre-card-number">3</span> Plan your TRE

Select the reference architecture and governance model that fit your use case, and adapt the provided
templates to your context.

</div>

<div class="tre-card tre-step-card">

### <span class="tre-card-number">4</span> Build, operate & connect

Follow the implementation guides to deploy, operate and connect your TRE to the wider EOSC ecosystem.

</div>

</div>
